# Curriculum & Geographic Standards Datasets

UnitWise incorporates standardized international geographic and curriculum data to provide neutral, accurate, and globally consistent educational planning.

## 1. Geographic Datasets (ISO Standards)

### ISO 3166-1 (Countries and Dependent Territories)
- **Dataset / Package:** `i18n-iso-countries`
- **Version:** `7.14.0`
- **License:** MIT License
- **Upstream Source:** ISO 3166-1 Maintenance Agency / Unicode CLDR
- **Usage:** Canonical ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 and alpha-3 codes, standard English names, common multilingual aliases (e.g., "Türkiye", "Bharat", "Great Britain").
- **Treatment:** Neutral representation of international states and recognized territories according to ISO standard nomenclature without political commentary.

### ISO 3166-2 (Country Subdivisions)
- **Dataset / Package:** `iso-3166-2`
- **Version:** `1.0.0`
- **License:** MIT License
- **Upstream Source:** ISO 3166-2 Maintenance Agency
- **Usage:** Standardized regional subdivision codes (e.g., `IN-GJ` for Gujarat, `US-CA` for California, `AU-NSW` for New South Wales) used for state, provincial, and territorial educational jurisdictions.

## 2. Educational Organizations & Curricula

All educational bodies and curricula in the UnitWise directory are recorded with:
1. **Official issuing body:** Ministry of education, state department, national examination board, or accredited international organization.
2. **Official website domain:** Authenticated web address verified via domain check.
3. **Verification status:**
   - `verified`: Directly verified against primary official ministry/board publications with verified domains.
   - `provisional`: Authentic established board undergoing syllabus document indexing.
   - `community_submitted`: Teacher-suggested board pending administrative review.
   - `unverified`: User-defined local framework (honest fallback).
4. **Primary source citations:** Direct links to public syllabus specifications and guidelines.
5. **Licensing & Copyright Compliance:** UnitWise does not distribute wholesale copies of proprietary curricular documents. Only public metadata, syllabus outlines, learning outcome codes, and short pedagogical excerpts under fair use are indexed.
